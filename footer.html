James Doy is a Product Designer. He creates user-focused digital experiences that solve real world problems.

His former careers include a year long stint as an apprentice fork-lift truck mechanic, a barman in the third most popular bar in Lowestoft and a brief, but eventful week as a New York City courier. Mostly though, he's been a designer since 2008.

James has worked both client and agency side with brands such as: BT, Argos, Post Office, Wickes, Office Depot, Tails.com, Mitsubishi Motors, HSBC, Sky, HERE Technologies and Your Parking Space.
